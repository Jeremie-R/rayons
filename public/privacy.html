Rayons privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Rayons is a personal, open-source app that shows your Gmail and Google Calendar organised around projects. It is run by Jérémie Roberrini. The source code is public at github.com/Jeremie-R/rayons.

What Rayons accesses in your Google account

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): to list and read your conversations, show attachments, apply the labels Rayons uses for tags and projects, mark mail read or unread, archive, move to trash or spam, send replies you write, and unsubscribe when you ask.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, calendar.calendarlist.readonly): to show your calendars and events, and create, change or delete events when you ask. A tag or project you give an event is stored on the event itself, as a private property only you can see.
  • Your name, email address and picture, to sign you in.

What Rayons stores

Your mail and events are not copied: they are read from Google when you open a screen and shown to you only. Rayons stores, in a database hosted by Supabase (EU):

  • the projects you create, their names and notes, and files you upload to them;
  • which Google account is connected, and the Google access and refresh tokens, encrypted, so Rayons can reach Gmail and Calendar for you.

Sharing

Rayons does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads your mail. The only services involved are Google (your data), Supabase (database and file storage), Vercel (hosting) and, only when you ask for a translation in a browser that can't translate on the device, DeepL (the text of that conversation is sent to translate it).

Rayons’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

In Rayons, You → Disconnect Google revokes Rayons’ access and deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove access at any time from your Google account permissions. To delete your projects, notes and files as well, write to the address below.
